About

Computer Science graduate from Princess Nourah bint Abdulrahman University, with hands-on experience building mobile applications in Flutter and Dart and designing user-centred interfaces in Figma.

I completed cooperative training at stc in partnership with Tata Consultancy Services, in the applications sector. I also took part in Tuwaiq Academy's intensive training camp in Emerging Technologies and Virtual Worlds, and co-developed Captain Crew, an immersive VR experience for Meta Quest 3.

Alongside my studies I led Google Developer Groups on Campus at PNU and served on the Student Council. I care about communication, leadership and continuous learning.
